Từ Vựng Lesson 1 (Places & Time)

Cấu Trúc Ngữ Pháp Lesson 1 (He / She - Was / Wasn't)

1. Hỏi & Trả Lời Nơi Ở Quá Khứ
Where was + he / she + yesterday?
➔ He / She was at the + [địa điểm].
Ví dụ 1: Where was he yesterday? ➔ He was at the aquarium.
Ví dụ 2: Where was she yesterday? ➔ She was at the beach.
2. Câu Hỏi Yes/No Với Was
Was + he / she + at the + [địa điểm] + yesterday?
➔ Yes, he/she was.
➔ No, he/she wasn't.
💡 Ghi nhớ cốt lõi:
• wasn't = was not
• Chủ ngữ số ít he / she / I ➔ dùng was / wasn't

Từ Vựng Lesson 2 (Shops & Places)

Cấu Trúc Ngữ Pháp Lesson 2 (They - Were / Weren't)

1. Hỏi Nhiều Người (They) Ở Đâu
Where were + they + yesterday?
➔ They were at the + [địa điểm].
Ví dụ: Where were they yesterday? ➔ They were at the bookstore.
2. Hỏi Yes/No Với Were
Were they + at the + [địa điểm] + yesterday?
➔ Yes, they were.
➔ No, they weren't. They were at the + [địa điểm đúng].
💡 Bảng So Sánh Chủ Ngữ:
• they / we / you ➔ dùng were / weren't
• he / she / I ➔ dùng was / wasn't
